Conductor in an Electric Field

By Dr. Gerard Joseph Lim

Learning Objectives & Guide

  • No Ionization: Conduction electrons are collectively redistributed, individual atoms do not ionize.
  • Electron Redistribution: Delocalized conduction electrons (blue) drift collectively opposite to the external electric field.
  • Surface Charges: Mobile charges pile up on boundaries, creating excess/deficit charge layers at the surfaces.
  • Field Cancellation: At electrostatic equilibrium, surface charges create an internal induced field (red) that perfectly cancels the external field (blue) inside.
For illustration purposes only - the electrons are not necessarily stripped off the atoms in the presence of an electric field.
