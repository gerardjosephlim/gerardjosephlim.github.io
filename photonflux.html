Learning Objectives & Guide

  • Flux Intensity: Adjust the Photon Flux slider to change the density of incoming solar photons (gold particles).
  • Panel Tilt Angle: Rotate the solar panel using the Tilt slider to observe how inclination changes its cross-section.
  • Effective Projected Area: Note the visual gold vector showing the projected width of the panel orthogonal to incoming rays.
  • Flux Formula: Witness the cos(θ) dependency (Φ = Φ₀ · cos(θ)) directly reflected in the ammeter and lightbulb glow!
