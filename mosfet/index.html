Lecture Overview & Instructions

  • Physical Cross-Section: Visualises donor/acceptor wells, expanding space-charge depletion boundaries, gate dielectric, and the mobile inversion channel.
  • Pinch-Off & Channel Length Modulation: Observe the channel pinching off when VDS ≥ VDS(sat) = VGS - VT, and watch ΔL shorten the conductive channel length.
  • NMOS vs PMOS & Modes: Switch between n⁺ - p - n⁺ and p⁺ - n - p⁺ across both Enhancement (normally-off) and Depletion (normally-on) modes.
  • Surface Energy Band Diagram: Track conduction (EC), intrinsic (Ei), and valence (EV) bands along the oxide-silicon surface with dynamic barrier lowering.
  • Synchronized I-V Curves: Family of output curves (ID vs VDS) and transfer curves (ID vs VGS) with live Q-point manipulation.
