Lab Guide & Overview

  • Four Synchronized Panels: Inspect (1) Band Dispersion E−k, (2) Density of States g(E), (3) Fermi–Dirac Probability f(E), and (4) State Occupation on one unified energy axis.
  • Exact Carrier Integrals: Electron (n) and hole (p) concentrations are computed via exact numerical quadrature of gc(E)f(E) and gv(E)[1−f(E)] across thermal tails.
  • Charge Neutrality: Equilibrium Fermi level EF is solved by bisection enforcing p+ND+−n−NA−=0.
  • Freeze-Out & Ionization: Toggle incomplete ionization to observe dopant freeze-out with finite donor (ED) and acceptor (EA) activation depths.
  • Shared Horizontal Scale: Panel 4 state distributions share a single, unnormalized physical scale (cm−3eV−1) with auto-fit or locked scale modes.
  • Dual Theme Engine: Toggle between Deep Space Dark Mode and Classic PC Beige Workstation via the header icon.
