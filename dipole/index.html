Dielectric Polarization Lab

Physical Principles

  • Applied Field (E₀): When a voltage is applied to the capacitor plates, free charges (±σf) create an external electric field E₀.
  • Dielectric Polarization: The electric field displaces positive nuclei and negative electron clouds, inducing atomic dipoles or aligning permanent molecular dipoles.
  • Bound Surface Charge (±σb): Dipole alignment cancels out in the bulk but leaves unneutralized bound charges ±σb = P at the dielectric surfaces.
  • Internal Screening: The bound charges set up an opposing depolarization field EP. The net field inside the dielectric is reduced to Enet = E₀ / κ.
  • Capacitance Gain: Because the internal field (and voltage) drops by a factor of κ, capacitance increases to C = κ · C₀.
