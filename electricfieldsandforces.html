Instructions

  • Add Charge: Click the "+ Add Charged Particle" button.
  • Move Charge: Drag the particles around the canvas.
  • Adjust Properties: Select a charge to change its magnitude and state.
  • Simulate: Click "Play" to observe dynamic behavior.
  • Field Probe: Toggle the probe to measure local field (N/C) and potential (V).
  • Gaussian Surface: Enclose charges to visualize flux (ΦE = Qenc/ε0) and count field line piercings.
  • Shortcuts: [Space] Play/Pause, [Del] Delete charge, [Arrows] Nudge, [G] Draw loop, [S] Sphere, [P] Probe, [1-4] Units.
