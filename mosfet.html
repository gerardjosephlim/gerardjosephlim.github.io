MOSFET Physics & Visualisation

By Dr. Gerard Joseph Lim

Lecture Overview & Instructions

  • Physical Cross-Section: Visualises donor/acceptor wells, expanding depletion boundaries, gate oxide, and the mobile inversion channel.
  • Pinch-Off & Channel Modulation: Observe the channel pinching off when VDS ≥ VDS(sat) = VGS - VT, and watch ΔL shorten the effective channel length.
  • NMOS vs PMOS & Modes: Switch between n⁺ - p - n⁺ and p⁺ - n - p⁺ across both Enhancement (normally-off) and Depletion (normally-on) modes.
  • Energy Band Diagram: Examine conduction (EC) and valence (EV) band bending along the channel and observe Drain-Induced Barrier Lowering (DIBL).
  • Synchronized I-V Curves: Family of output curves (ID vs VDS) and transfer curves (ID vs VGS) with live Q-point tracking.
