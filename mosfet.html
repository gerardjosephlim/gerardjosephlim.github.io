MOSFET Physics & Visualisation

By Dr. Gerard Joseph Lim

Lecture Overview & Instructions

  • Physical Cross-Section: Visualises donor/acceptor wells, expanding depletion boundaries, gate oxide, and the mobile inversion channel.
  • Pinch-Off & Channel Modulation: Observe the channel pinching off when $V_{DS} \ge V_{DS(sat)} = V_{GS} - V_T$, and watch $\Delta L$ shorten the effective channel length.
  • NMOS vs PMOS & Modes: Switch between $n^+ \text{-} p \text{-} n^+$ and $p^+ \text{-} n \text{-} p^+$ across both Enhancement (normally-off) and Depletion (normally-on) modes.
  • Energy Band Diagram: Examine conduction ($E_C$) and valence ($E_V$) band bending along the channel and observe Drain-Induced Barrier Lowering (DIBL).
  • Synchronized I-V Curves: Family of output curves ($I_D \text{ vs } V_{DS}$) and transfer curves ($I_D \text{ vs } V_{GS}$) with live Q-point tracking.
